FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything brides ask before booking — answered honestly.

HD makeup uses light-diffusing pigments applied by hand for a flawless, richly pigmented finish ideal for 4K video and ultra-close photography. Airbrush uses a spray gun for micro-thin layers — nearly weightless, dewy, and buildable — perfect for outdoor events. Both offer all-day longevity. We decide together at your trial.
Yes — every bridal package includes a dedicated 2–3 hour trial session, ideally 4–8 weeks before your wedding. We experiment with the exact look, discuss your outfit colours, test product longevity, and make adjustments. You leave with reference photos and complete confidence.
Travel within Mumbai is complimentary. Outstation bookings within Maharashtra attract a nominal travel charge. For destination weddings — Goa, Jaipur, Udaipur, abroad — travel, accommodation and local transport are added at actuals. I'll provide a clear all-inclusive quote with no hidden costs.
Wedding season slots (October–February) fill 6–12 months in advance. I recommend booking at least 6 months before your date. A 30% advance payment secures your date. Off-season dates can also fill unexpectedly — reach out now to check availability.
I use MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, NARS, Kryolan, Airbase and Dermacol — all professional-grade and dermatologically tested. If you have known allergies, mention them at enquiry. I do a patch test during the trial and can substitute products as needed.
Absolutely. I offer group packages for bridesmaids, mothers of the bride, and family members. For larger parties (4+ people), I can arrange a second qualified artist. Group rates are available — mention this when enquiring.
