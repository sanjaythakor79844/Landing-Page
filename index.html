About Kajol

Bridal Makeup Artist
& Academy, Mumbai

Kajol R Paswwan and our team believe that every bride deserves to feel like the best version of herself on her special day. Makeup is not merely about enhancing features — it is about capturing the essence of each bride and illuminating her inner glow.

Our process begins by truly listening to the bride's vision. We understand that every detail matters, from the desired style to the intricate themes of the wedding, and we are dedicated to formulating a look that beautifully reflects her personality, complements her outfit, and aligns with the wedding's overall aesthetic.

Having had the privilege to be part of thousands of bridal journeys, we cherish every moment shared with our brides. The trust they place in us — especially during such a significant and emotional time — is immense.
